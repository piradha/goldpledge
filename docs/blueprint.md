# **App Name**: PledgeVault

## Core Features:

- Login & Role-Based Redirection: Secure login with email/mobile and password, with OTP-based password recovery and role-based redirection.
- Dashboard Overview: Display key metrics such as total gold weight pledged, total pledge amount, new pledges, pending releases, and overdue accounts.
- Customer Management: Manage customer profiles with name, mobile number, ID, address, photo, and notes. Send SMS reminders for due alerts.
- New Pledge Entry: Create pledge entries with customer selection/addition, item details (karat, type, weight, value), and loan details (amount, interest, duration, due date).
- Automatic Calculation of Financial Metrics: The app uses a tool which takes item values, weights, loan specifics and payment dates to produce estimates of the expected interest per month and the total amount payable to date.
- Pledge Record View: Display pledge details with auto-incremented pledge number, customer details, item photo, loan amount, remaining interest, and status. Actions include making payment, editing, and printing receipts.
- Payment & Renewal: Process monthly interest, partial payments, and full settlements with late fee calculation and new due date generation. Generate printed PDF and WhatsApp/SMS receipts.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep gold (#D4AF37) to evoke a sense of value and trust.
- Background color: Dark, desaturated gray (#212529) to create a premium feel and contrast with gold elements.
- Accent color: Light gold (#E6C466) to highlight key interactive elements and CTAs.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif font with a modern, machined, objective, neutral look.
- Use simple, line-based icons in a gold color to maintain a consistent and elegant look.
- Employ a minimal layout with clear section divisions to enhance readability and user experience.
- Subtle transitions and animations to provide feedback on user interactions without overwhelming the interface.
